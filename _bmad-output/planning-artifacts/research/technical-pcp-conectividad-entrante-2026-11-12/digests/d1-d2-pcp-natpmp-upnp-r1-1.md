# Digest: Inbound-connectivity protocols (PCP / NAT-PMP / UPnP-IGD) for IPv6 firewall pinholes — real-world CPE support

**Access date for all sources: 2026-11-12. Caveat: `webfetch` was not available in this session; all source content comes from `web_search` result text (titles, URLs, substantial excerpts), not full-page fetches. Treat quoted snippets as search-extracted excerpts.**

---

## TL;DR for the decision

IPv6 firewall *pinholes* (open inbound to a GUA without NAT) are achievable today through **two real mechanisms**: **PCP MAP over IPv6 transport** and **UPnP IGDv2 `WANIPv6FirewallControl::AddPinhole`**. But they are deployed on only a narrow slice of CPE: **AVM Fritz!Box** (both protocols, verified working by third parties) and **miniupnpd-based firmware** (OpenWrt, pfSense/OPNsense, Turris, Asuswrt-Merlin, Gargoyle, Ubiquiti EdgeRouter). NAT-PMP **cannot** open IPv6 pinholes — it is IPv4-only by design. Most stock ISP/consumer CPE (Comcast, Vodafone Station, TP-Link, Netgear, MikroTik, Freebox, Japanese domestic routers) offer no IPv6-pinhole protocol at all. Counterpoint: on several of those (Freebox, Netgear passthrough, firewalls disabled by default), inbound IPv6 is *fully open* anyway, so no protocol is needed — support should be probed, not assumed absent-or-present.

## Contradictions (both sides cited)

1. **ASUS + PCP.** SNBForums (2013): ASUS miniupnpd responds "unsupported version" to PCP v2 — no PCP. Radicle commit (~2025): "miniupnpd-based routers (OpenWRT, pfSense, **Asus**, Ubiquiti) dispatch NAT-PMP and PCP off the same socket". Resolution hypothesis: Merlin builds include PCP; stock ASUSWRT historically didn't. Current stock status unresolved — SNBForums 2025-2026 reports stock UPnP outright broken on 3.0.0.6.102-era firmware anyway. **Treat ASUS stock PCP as unverified/likely-absent; Merlin as present.**
2. **"PCP is a successor worth using" vs. deployment reality.** RFC/WG charter positions PCP for CGN+CPE; heimnetz forum + JANOG49 data show near-zero ISP server deployment and near-zero domestic CPE support outside Fritz!Box/miniupnpd world. Both true: spec is sound, deployment is thin.
3. **Freebox UPnP "works" vs. pinholes.** Freebox answers UPnP IGDv2 and IPv4 port mappings work, but `WANIPv6FirewallControl` reports pinholes not allowed — spec-compliant refusal, not an implementation bug.

## Not found (absence of evidence)

- **No systematic PCP deployment measurement** — no internet scan or academic study quantifying PCP-server prevalence on CPE found (searches for scans returned NAT-PMP/UPnP studies only; PCP gets lumped into port-5351 reporting).
- **No evidence of PCP on Netgear, TP-Link, D-Link, Linksys, Google/Nest Wifi, eero** — eero exposes only a UPnP toggle (per Edovia docs).
- **No evidence of ANY US/UK ISP-supplied CPE supporting IPv6 pinholes** (Comcast, Vodafone, Sky checked — UPnP-IPv4 only at best, disabled by default).
- **No evidence Fritz!Box PCP supports THIRD_PARTY or FILTER options** — only MAP-pinhole behavior evidenced.
- **No evidence WANIPv6FirewallControl works over IPv6 SSDP reliably in the field** — OpenWrt tracker notes IPv6 GUA SSDP announcements are disabled when IPv6 arrives after IPv4 (a real discovery-path bug).
- **Freebox FS#37515 status today** — bug from ~2021; whether current Freebox OS still returns InboundPinholeAllowed=0 unverified.

## Bottom-line recommendation input

For serverless P2P on residential IPv6: implement **PCP MAP-over-IPv6 first** (covers Fritz!Box — the biggest pinhole-capable installed base — plus all miniupnpd CPEs with one wire protocol), **WANIPv6FirewallControl AddPinhole second** (miniupnpd + Fritz!Box again, plus linuxigd-based boxes), and treat NAT-PMP/UPnP-IGDv1 as IPv4-only fallbacks. Expect coverage to be *minority* of CPEs; design must degrade gracefully to "IPv6 firewall probably open or unfixably closed" detection, since several popular CPEs (Freebox, Netgear passthrough, disabled-by-default firewalls) need no protocol at all while others (MikroTik, UniFi, ISP boxes) can never satisfy the request.
